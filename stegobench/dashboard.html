    How the scores are calculated

    Recovery = chance-adjusted bit recovery of the framed payload after the attack and error correction (50 % match = 0 %, 100 % = exact). Robustness = mean recovery over all supported attacks.

    Imperceptibility = 70 % PSNR (30 dB → 0, 60 dB → 100) + 30 % SSIM (11×11 Gaussian window; 0.95 → 0, 1.00 → 100).

    Stealth = 100 − detection risk. With fewer than 12 image-key samples the risk is a heuristic: 30 % visible distortion, 50 % strongest of RS / Weighted Stego / progressive chi-square (each measured against the cover), 20 % growth of high-frequency noise. With 12 or more samples it comes from the measured ROC area of the best classical detector: stealth = 100 × (1 − max(0, 2·AUC − 1)).

    Capacity = log-scaled maximum payload. Overall = weighted sum with the weights of the selected goal. “#1 in x %” re-ranks 600 random weightings to show how much the winner depends on the weights you chose.

    RS, WS and chi-square target LSB replacement. They are classical detectors, not trained steganalysers (SRM, SRNet): treat “undetected” as “not caught by these tests”. See the README for cross-checking with Aletheia.
